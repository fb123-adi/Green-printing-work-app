Assumptions
• Nocturne dark theme, tuned for Android. Touch targets are at least 44px.
• The Director account has a private ID. It needs its password plus a 6-digit code (2FA). Department heads also use 2FA. After 5 wrong passwords, login locks for 60s. The Director is logged out after 15 minutes idle.
• Timelines are written by the system and can't be edited. Submitting work is not approval: work goes Working → Submitted → Approved or Correction.
• Staff and work data exports as a multi-sheet Excel file from Me → Data & records.
